About the Admission Dashboard

The Admission Dashboard provides a complete overview of the student admission process in one place. It helps administrators manage applications, monitor admission status, track fee payments, and generate detailed reports with ease. The dashboard offers real-time updates, making it simple to review student information, approve applications, and stay informed about important activities. With an organized and user-friendly interface, it improves productivity and ensures a smooth admission experience for both staff and students.
